☠ Pirate Fleet

Sail: W/S throttle, A/D turn. Crew: left-click a pirate, then right-click a cannon to man it. Press L to auto-load, R to auto-repair, B to bail, N for the crow's nest, and Tab to swap the gun crews to the other side. Mines: press M on three pirates (or right-click the stern dropper): the first two man it, the third loads it. Then press X to drop a mine behind you. Oars: press O on a pirate (or right-click an oar seat near the bow); each manned oar adds speed. Wheel zooms, drag or arrow keys look around. Space fires every manned, loaded cannon. 1000 gal sinks you; last ship afloat wins.
